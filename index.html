Who We Are

India's Trusted Insurance Investigation Partner

Bima Anveshak is a specialist insurance claims investigation agency with over a decade of experience serving India's top insurers. Our team combines legal expertise, field intelligence, and data analytics to deliver investigations that are thorough, timely, and legally admissible.

From motor theft and OD claims to health, fire, and liability — we investigate every type of claim with the same rigour. Headquartered in Mumbai with field teams across 12 states.

  • LLB-qualified investigation leadership
  • Multilingual: Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Tamil
  • Former senior investigators from leading PSU & private insurers
  • Pan-India network of 200+ trained field investigators

What We Do

Our Services

Comprehensive investigation services across all lines of insurance business

🚗

Motor Claim Investigation

Own damage, theft, total loss, accidental claims — we verify at scene, check RTO records, and assess genuineness from accident to repair.

🏥

Health Claim Investigation

Cashless and reimbursement claim verification — hospital credentialing, document authenticity checks, pre-existing condition investigation.

🔥

Fire & Property Investigation

Origin and cause analysis, stock verification, forensic liaison, and occupancy verification for fire and burglary claims.

🛡️

Fraud Detection & Analytics

Pattern analysis, network link mapping, and market intelligence to identify organised fraud rings before claims are settled.

📷

Surveillance

Discreet, legally compliant physical and digital surveillance with professionally recorded, time-stamped evidence reports.

📋

Background Verification

Deep-dive verification of claimants, insured entities, hospitals, garages, and third parties — including CIBIL, litigation, and criminal history checks.
