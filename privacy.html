Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Bima Anveshak ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information in connection with our insurance investigation services.

1. Who We Are

Bima Anveshak is an independent insurance claims investigation agency headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. We provide investigation, surveillance, and verification services exclusively to licensed insurers and intermediaries regulated by the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI).

2. Information We Collect

In the course of conducting insurance investigations we may collect, or receive from commissioning insurers, the following categories of personal data:

  • Full name, date of birth, and government-issued identification numbers (Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence, passport)
  • Contact details – address, telephone numbers, email address
  • Medical records and health information (for health/life claim investigations)
  • Vehicle registration and insurance details (for motor investigations)
  • Financial and employment records where relevant to claim verification
  • Photographic and video evidence collected during field investigations or surveillance
  • Witness and third-party statements

When you contact us via our website contact form or chatbot, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and the content of your enquiry.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data on the following lawful bases under applicable Indian law and in alignment with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA):

  • Contractual necessity – to perform investigation services contracted by insurers
  • Legitimate interests – fraud prevention, regulatory compliance, and protection of the insurance ecosystem
  • Legal obligation – to comply with IRDAI regulations, court orders, or law-enforcement requests
  • Consent – where you have provided explicit consent for specific processing activities

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Conducting insurance claim investigations and preparing factual investigation reports
  • Verifying the authenticity of claims and detecting fraudulent activity
  • Communicating with you in relation to a specific investigation or general enquiry
  • Improving our services and website
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Investigation reports and any personal data therein are shared exclusively with the commissioning insurer or their authorised representative. We do not sell, trade, or rent personal information to third parties.

We may disclose information to:

  • Regulatory and law-enforcement authorities when legally required
  • Courts and tribunals pursuant to valid legal process
  • Third-party service providers (e.g. cloud storage, email delivery) bound by confidentiality obligations

6. Data Retention

Investigation case files and related personal data are retained for a minimum of 7 years from case closure, in line with IRDAI record-keeping requirements and applicable limitation periods. Website enquiry data is retained for up to 2 years.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include encrypted storage, access controls, and staff confidentiality obligations.

8. Your Rights

Under the DPDPA 2023 and applicable Indian law, you may have rights to:

  • Access personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure of data (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Nominate a person to exercise rights on your behalf

To exercise any right, please email info@bimaanveshak.com with your request. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics (GA4) to collect anonymised, aggregated data about visitor behaviour (pages visited, session duration, device type). No personally identifiable information is transmitted to Google Analytics. You may opt out by using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
