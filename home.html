FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

We work exclusively with licensed insurance companies, TPAs (Third Party Administrators), and authorised intermediaries. We do not accept direct mandates from individual policyholders or claimants.

Use the contact form below or email us at info@bimaanveshak.com with the claim details, policy number, and scope of investigation required. Our team will respond within one business day.

Our primary coverage is Gujarat with headquarters in Ahmedabad. We currently cover 12 states across India including Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Delhi NCR, Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, Haryana and more. Contact us for specific location enquiries.

Bima Anveshak is an independent investigation agency operating in compliance with IRDAI's guidelines for insurance investigation. All investigations are conducted according to IRDAI regulations and the DPDPA 2023 data protection framework.

Timelines vary by claim type and complexity. Standard investigations are completed and reported within the agreed turnaround time specified in the service agreement with each insurer. Complex or multi-location cases may require additional time.

All personal data is handled strictly in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDPA). Investigation reports are shared only with the commissioning insurer. We maintain strict confidentiality across all case files. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
